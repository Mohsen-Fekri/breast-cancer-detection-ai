# Sample images

Six breast ultrasound images from the held-out **BreastMNIST test split** (MedMNIST v2), which the bundled model never saw during training: three labelled malignant and three labelled normal or benign.

BreastMNIST is derived from the Breast Ultrasound Images (BUSI) dataset and is released under CC BY 4.0. The images are de-identified research data.

- Yang, J. et al. MedMNIST v2: A large-scale lightweight benchmark for 2D and 3D biomedical image classification. *Scientific Data* 10, 41 (2023).
- Al-Dhabyani, W. et al. Dataset of breast ultrasound images. *Data in Brief* 28, 104863 (2020).
